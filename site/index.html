    Built to act, not only answer

    One loop owns everything: listen, transcribe, decide, act, speak. Every part of it closes the gap between saying something and it being done, without handing the model the keys.

    Full-duplex voice

    Say "E.V." once and keep talking. Talk over a reply and it stops mid-word to listen. It learns your voice and its own, so it never answers itself on loudspeakers.

    Eyes and hands for any app

    The window list and the accessibility tree first, screenshots only when those cannot do the job. Every click gets a second look.

    Runs in about 120 MB

    No local model, no vendor SDKs, no audio libraries. The brain, the ears and the voice are plain HTTP calls on one warm connection.

    Guardrails that assume the model is wrong

    Anything destructive or that spends money asks "Confirm?" first. "Stop everything" or Ctrl+Alt+Q locks it down, and only your voice can unlock it.
